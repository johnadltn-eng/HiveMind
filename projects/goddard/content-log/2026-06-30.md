3 Wesley Chapel spots your toddler will talk about for a week

**SLIDE 2 — The list**
**PopStroke** — Tiger Woods mini-golf with a shaded playground and ice cream on-site. Two 18-hole courses, totally doable with little ones. Book a weekday tee time to skip the crowd.

**Epperson Lagoon** — 7 acres of crystal-clear water and a floating obstacle course. Your 5-year-old will think you're the coolest parent alive.

**Cypress Creek Preserve** — Free, shaded trails wide enough for a stroller. Best-kept secret in Pasco County for a morning out that doesn't feel like a chore.
