Goddard School Wesley Chapel · Social content strategy, cadence, and workflow
The Goal
Instagram is your front door for local parents. The strategy is simple: show up consistently in Wesley Chapel parents' feeds so that when they're ready to choose a school, Goddard is already familiar and trusted.
Every piece of content we create is designed to reach parents of children ages 0–6 in the Wesley Chapel area — before they're even actively searching. Organic reach now builds the enrollment pipeline later.

The Cadence
Daily

Local News Carousel

One Wesley Chapel carousel post per day. Curated local content — events, spots for kids, community news relevant to parents of young children. No negative stories, no generic content.

Weekly

Strategy Call + Report

Weekly call with Ohm to review performance, align on upcoming content, and document decisions. A written summary goes to the directors after every call.

Monthly

On-Site Shoot

One on-site visit to batch 3–4 reels of real school footage. This gives us a month of school content from a single shoot — efficient and consistent.

Content Rules
We Post This
  • Local Wesley Chapel events and activities
  • Content relevant to parents of kids ages 0–6
  • Real footage from inside the school
  • Collabs with trusted local businesses
  • Proven posts from high-performing Goddard accounts
  • Positive, community-forward stories
We Avoid This
  • Generic parenting content with no local angle
  • Negative news stories or safety concerns
  • Hard sales posts or enrollment pressure
  • Content that doesn't reflect the Wesley Chapel community
  • Posts without a clear parent audience in mind
Approval Process
One revision per video, then it posts. This keeps the content moving and avoids back-and-forth that stalls momentum. Carousels are reviewed before going live. Reels get one round of feedback — if there are changes, they get made once, then the post goes out.
If the school has footage to share (events, classroom moments, holiday content), upload it to the shared Google Drive folder. We'll edit, add the Goddard logo, and schedule it. No need to coordinate — just drop it in the folder.
