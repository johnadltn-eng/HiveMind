# Goddard News Carousel

## Trigger
John runs this each morning, or says "run the news search."

## Steps

### Step 1 — Search
Search for Wesley Chapel, FL news from the last 24-48 hours.

Filter for relevance to parents of young children ages 0-6:
- Local events and community programs
- School and education news
- Family-friendly new businesses
- Road closures affecting school routes
- Weather alerts

Exclude: crime, accidents, anything negative, anything unrelated to family life.

Before surfacing any story, ask: "Would a Wesley Chapel mom aged 28-40 stop scrolling for this?" If not, cut it.

### Step 2 — Candidates
Return 3-5 candidate stories. For each:

**[Headline]**
Summary: [one sentence]
Source: [link]
Carousel angle: [one sentence — how to frame this for a Goddard parent, the hook for slide 1]

### Step 3 — Once John picks a story
Draft carousel copy in this format:

**Slide 1 — Hook**
[Headline / scroll-stopper, 1 line]

**Slide 2 — Summary**
[2-3 sentences, plain language, parent-friendly]

**Slide 3 — CTA**
[Soft, community-feel CTA — e.g., "Tag a Wesley Chapel parent who needs to know this"]

Output must be copy-paste ready into Canva. Zero editing required.

### Step 4 — Save
Save the chosen story + carousel copy to `projects/goddard/content-log/YYYY-MM-DD.md` using today's date.
