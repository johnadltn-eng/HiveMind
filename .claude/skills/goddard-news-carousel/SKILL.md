# Goddard News Carousel

## Trigger
John runs this each morning, or says "run the news search" or "run the carousel."

## Brand
- Colors: Navy #1B2F4E, White #FFFFFF, Sky Blue accent #7EB8F7
- Logo: projects/goddard/Goddard-logo.png (circular, navy + white horse)
- Format: 5-slide Instagram carousel, 1080x1080px

---

## Step 1 — Search

Search for Wesley Chapel, FL content from the last 24-48 hours.

Prioritize:
- Local spots, hidden gems, things to do with young kids
- Community events relevant to families
- New family-friendly businesses opening
- Local lifestyle content (coffee shops, parks, trails, restaurants with kids menus)
- Seasonal or timely content (summer activities, holiday events, back to school)

Filter out:
- Crime, accidents, anything negative
- Anything not relevant to parents of kids ages 0-6
- Salesy or promotional content (we surface community value, not ads)

Before surfacing any story, ask: **"Would a Wesley Chapel mom aged 28-40 stop scrolling for this?"** If not, cut it.

---

## Step 2 — Candidates

Return 3 candidates. For each:

**[Topic/Location Name]**
- Why a Wesley Chapel mom stops for this: [one sentence]
- Carousel angle: [the hook — what makes this post worth posting]
- Source: [link]

Then ask John to pick one.

---

## Step 4 — Save

Save the full output to `projects/goddard/content-log/YYYY-MM-DD.md` using today's date.

---

## Constraints
- One topic per slide — never crowd multiple spots onto one slide
- Description lines max 10 words — if it's longer, cut it
- The hook slide headline should make someone stop mid-scroll
- Never negative, never salesy, never generic ("check out this great place!")
- Always sounds like a local who actually lives there, not a brand
