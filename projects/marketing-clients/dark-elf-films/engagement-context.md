## Working principles for how this engagement runs
1. **Discovery happens before sessions, not during them.** Sessions are for building documents live, not interviewing the client — that's why the platform review happens ahead of Session 1.
2. **Anchor the rollout plan to the launch date, working backward** — what has to post day-of, what builds anticipation the week before, what needs to restart now.
3. **Separate "what to post" from "how it performs."** Scoped to deliver the plan, not promise outcomes — stay consistent with the no-guarantee clause.
4. **Hold scope discipline live, not just on paper.** If Jonathan, V, or Hannah asks for something outside the 5 sessions mid-call, the move is: "that's outside these sessions, let's scope it separately after" — not answer it in the room.
5. **Insights go in the paid sessions, not in casual pre-session messages.** Platform-review notes stay internal until the session, rather than giving away the analysis for free beforehand.
